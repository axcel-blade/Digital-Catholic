# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

Digital Catholic is a resource for learning and sharing the Catholic faith. Everyone who contributes — whether submitting content, code, images, or feedback — is expected to act with charity, respect, and honesty.

## Standards

**Expected behaviour:**

- Communicate charitably and respectfully in all project spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions)
- Keep content accurate and aligned with Catholic teaching and tradition
- Accept constructive feedback graciously
- Acknowledge others' contributions fairly

**Unacceptable behaviour:**

- Disrespectful, abusive, or demeaning language toward any person
- Content that contradicts or undermines Catholic doctrine
- Plagiarism or submission of copyrighted material without proper attribution
- Spam, self-promotion, or off-topic content
- Harassment of any kind

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to all project spaces: GitHub issues, pull requests, discussions, and any other forum associated with this repository.

## Enforcement

Violations may result in removal of content, rejection of contributions, or blocking from the project at the maintainers' discretion.

To report an issue, open a private issue on GitHub or contact the maintainer directly.

## Grounding

This project is made in the spirit of service. Contributors are asked to approach their work as one would approach any act of faith — with humility, care, and a desire to serve others well.
